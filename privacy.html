Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Betternome is made by Moxie App Design. This policy covers the Betternome iPhone app, the Betternome web app at moxieappdesign.com/betternome/app, and this website. The short version: there's no account, no ads and no tracking, and your music stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Betternome has no account and no servers of its own. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Your songs and recordings

  • Audio files you load and anything you record with Record are analyzed right on your device to find the tempo and the drum hits. They are never uploaded.
  • The microphone is used only while you're recording a take. Betternome asks for permission first, and you can turn it off any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • MIDI files you save go only where you send them, through the share sheet or your downloads.

Settings and setlists

Your tempo, time signatures, sounds, setlists, loops, color and other settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes them.

Song search and services you connect

These features are optional, and they send only what's needed to the service named:

  • Song search and Spotify links (iPhone app): the song name or link you enter is sent to Deezer's public catalog, and for a Spotify link to Spotify, to look up the song and its tempo.
  • Apple Music (iPhone app): if you connect Apple Music, iOS asks for your permission and Apple's MusicKit handles search and playback. We never see your Apple ID or your library.
  • Spotify (iPhone app): if you connect Spotify, you sign in on Spotify's own page. Betternome can then see what's playing and play, pause and loop it. The sign-in token is stored in your iPhone's secure keychain and is never sent to us. You can disconnect in the app or in your Spotify account settings.

Those services have their own privacy policies: Apple, Spotify and Deezer.

Purchases

Betternome Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is owned on your Apple Account.

The web app and this website

The web app and betternome.app are pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run the service, and the web app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Betternome itself does not set cookies.

Children

Betternome doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
