Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Betternome is a metronome. It doesn't need to know who you are, and it doesn't try to. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your tempo, settings, setlists, loops and color choice are saved on your iPhone only.
  • Audio files you open and songs you record are analyzed on your iPhone. They aren't uploaded anywhere.
  • The microphone is only used while you tap Record, to find the tempo of a song playing nearby.
  • MIDI files you make are saved or shared only where you choose to send them.

Apple Music

If you connect Apple Music, Betternome uses Apple's MusicKit to search for songs and play them inside the app. Apple handles that, under Apple's privacy policy. Betternome only keeps the song you picked and your loop points on your iPhone.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, you sign in on Spotify's own page. Betternome stores the sign-in token in your iPhone's secure keychain and uses it only to see what's playing and to play, pause and loop it. Spotify's use of your data is covered by Spotify's privacy policy. You can disconnect anytime in your Spotify account settings.

Song tempo lookups

When you search for a song, Betternome sends the song's name, or its standard recording code, to Deezer's public music database to look up its BPM. Nothing that identifies you is included.

Purchases

Betternome Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Betternome never sees your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is owned on your Apple Account.

Children

Betternome doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email support@betternome.app.
