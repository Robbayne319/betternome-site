What it does

Everything you need to practice and play

Find the BPM

Tap along, analyze an audio file, record the song playing nearby, or search Apple Music and Spotify.

Loop the hard part

Tap IN and OUT, or pick 1 to 16 bars. Loops always land on the bar line, so the click stays in time every pass.

Slow it down

Play a song file at your tempo. Keep the pitch, or let it shift like tape. Apple Music and Spotify play at their own speed.

Train your time

Ramp the tempo up bar by bar, or drop the click out with Gaps and Random to test your inner clock.

Setlists

Save each song's tempo, time signature, sound and loop. Step through the set with one tap.

Any time signature

Pick one or make your own: 7/8, 5/4, groupings like 2+2+3, and the accents where you want them.

Learn any song

Loop it. Slow it. Nail it.

  • Play + click puts the metronome right on top of the song.
  • Drag IN and OUT a beat at a time to frame the exact passage.
  • Follow metronome (audio files): tap or drag the tempo and the song follows, from half to double speed.
  • Pick the pulse and see the tempo over time, so you know where a song speeds up.
